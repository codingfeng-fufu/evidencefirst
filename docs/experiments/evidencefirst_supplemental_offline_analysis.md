## Paper-Safe Claims

- EvidenceFirst v6 is strongest on HotpotQA bridge questions among the strongest
  completed graph baselines.
- EvidenceFirst v6 has positive unique-win balance against each completed
  baseline, but this should remain secondary to the main EM/F1 table.
- The final selector is a useful audit/calibration signal because selected
  answers are substantially more accurate than non-selected answers.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim EvidenceFirst dominates all question types.
- Do not claim selector calibration replaces controlled ablation.
- Do not claim diagnostic gap flags are a strong universal failure predictor;
  they are mixed outside disconnected/short-chain cases.

## Usage Boundary

EvidenceFirst usage summaries are available for the reported full artifacts:
HotpotQA v4/source-selector averages 5.711 LLM calls and 7095.1 tokens per
question, and 2Wiki v6 averages 5.938 calls and 6540.4 tokens. Baseline usage
artifacts are incomplete and `total_cost` fields are zero, so the paper should
not claim comparable monetary cost.

## Fallback Boundary

Fallback is implemented as a logged empty-triples path, but the final benchmark
artifacts barely exercise it: HotpotQA v4+selector has 5 fallback rows out of
1000, and 2Wiki v6 has 0 fallback rows out of 500. The implementation-level
stress test `test_empty_triples_use_passage_fallback_and_record_step` verifies
that an empty evidence-triple state uses passage fallback, skips the graph
reader, and records `no_triples_fallback`. This supports fallback traceability,
not fallback robustness under realistic sparse-Web conditions.
