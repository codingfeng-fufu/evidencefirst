## Scope And Acceptance Criteria

This plan addresses the four simulated official reviewer clusters:

- R1: Track fit / novelty risk.
- R2: Method soundness / implementation alignment risk.
- R3: Experiment fairness / artifact-claim alignment risk.
- R4: Writing density / LNCS polish risk.

The repair is complete when:

- `paper/evidencefirst_submission.tex` explicitly scopes the paper as a fixed-context Web Knowledge-RAG / GraphRAG pipeline contribution, not a live-Web or dynamic-KG claim.
- The method section includes a formal state-machine table or equivalent precise state-contract description.
- The paper no longer claims that diagnostic records are directly consumed by the reader unless the text says exactly how the current implementation uses state.
- Main result language is consistently “competitive under saved protocols,” not leaderboard superiority.
- The 2Wiki reader-full/local-context boundary is visible in both setup and result interpretation.
- The abstract and artifact prose are less dense and less defensive.
- The PDF compiles to 15 pages or fewer with no undefined references.
- `python scripts/verify_paper_artifacts.py --check-only` passes.
